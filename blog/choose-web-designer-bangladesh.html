How to choose a web designer in Bangladesh

By Rayan · 26 November 2026 · 4 min read

Type "best web designer in Bangladesh" into Google and you will see long lists of agencies and freelancers, all calling themselves the best. The honest truth is: there is no official ranking of web designers in Bangladesh. Nobody gives out a "number 1" title. So how do you choose? Judge by things you can check yourself.

1. Click real work, not screenshots

A screenshot proves nothing. Ask to click a live website the designer built — open the menu, try the cart, fill in the form. If it works smoothly on your phone, that is real skill. My portfolio has four working demos you can try right now: a restaurant site with ordering, a salon site with booking, a coaching centre site with admission forms, and a fashion shop with checkout.

2. Get a fixed written quote

Before any work starts, the price should be written down: what is included, how many pages, how many days, and what you pay when. A fair price in Bangladesh is a landing page from ৳2,500, a business website from ৳6,500, and an online shop from ৳9,000. You pay 50% to start and 50% only when the site is live and you approve it.

3. Check the delivery time

A standard business website should take 3–5 days, a landing page 2–3 days, an online shop 5–7 days. If someone promises a full website in one day, ask what is missing. And ask for a live preview link early, so you can follow the progress and ask for changes as it is built.

4. Make sure it is mobile-first

Most customers in Bangladesh search on their phone first, often on slow mobile data. A good website is designed for the small screen first, loads fast, and has click-to-call and WhatsApp buttons where customers expect them. Open the designer's own site on your phone — if it is slow or broken there, your site will be too.

5. Ask about support after launch

A website is not finished on launch day. You will want to change a price, add a photo, or fix something. Ask: do I get a guide to update the site myself, and how many days of free support are included? My sites include a written guide and 14 days of free support — 30 days on shop plans.

6. Ignore "best" and "top" claims — check reviews instead

Anyone can write "best web designer" on their own website. What matters is what customers say: Google reviews, Fiverr or Upwork ratings, and real businesses you can call. A designer with a few honest reviews is more trustworthy than one with a big "number 1" claim and no proof.
