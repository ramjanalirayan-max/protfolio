What changes the price?

  • Number of pages. A 5-page site costs less than a 12-page site. My business package includes up to 8 pages; extra pages are +৳600 each.
  • Features. A photo gallery (+৳800), a booking system (+৳1,500), online payment (+৳2,500) or two languages, Bangla + English (+৳1,500), add to the base price.
  • Content. If you give me your photos, prices and text, the work is faster. If I have to write the text for you, writing help is +৳600.
  • Design. A clean template-based design costs less than a fully custom design made from scratch for your brand.

What should be included in the price?

A fair quote in Bangladesh should include: a mobile-first design (most of your customers will visit on a phone), fast loading on mobile data, WhatsApp ordering or booking where it fits, a Google Map with your opening hours, a contact form, and basic SEO so Google can find the site. After launch you should get a short guide so you can change text, prices and photos yourself — my sites include 14 days of free support.

Watch out for these

  • No fixed quote. If the price is not written down before work starts, it can grow later. Always ask for a written quote.
  • Monthly lock-in. A normal business website is a one-time payment. Be careful if someone charges you every month just to keep the site online.
  • No live preview. You should see a live preview link before you pay the final 50%. If you cannot see the work, do not pay for it.

How to compare quotes

Do not compare only the lowest number. Ask every designer the same three questions: can I click a live website you built, what exactly is included in this price, and how many days until it is live? The answers tell you more than the price alone.
